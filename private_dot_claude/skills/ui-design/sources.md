# Sources

Read on 2026-10-10. Each line says what the skill took from it.

- [Visual Hierarchy in UX: Definition](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/),
    NN/g.
    The definition, three sizes at most, two large elements, grouping by
    proximity and common region, and the squint test
- [The Principles of Visual Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/),
    NN/g.
    Scale, hierarchy, balance, contrast, and Gestalt as the five levers
- [Progressive Disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/),
    NN/g.
    The definition, the primary and secondary split, staged disclosure, and the
    two-level ceiling
- [The Principle of Common Region](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/common-region/),
    NN/g.
    Whitespace before borders, and the question to ask before adding a box
- [F-Shaped Pattern of Reading on the Web](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/),
    NN/g.
    The scan patterns (F, layer-cake, spotted, marking, bypassing, commitment)
    and front-loading
- [Tooltip Guidelines](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tooltip-guidelines/), NN/g.
    Never task-critical, hover and focus, consistent across instances
- [10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/),
    NN/g.
    Visibility of status, match the reader's language, recognition over recall,
    shortcuts for experts, minimalist design, help in context
- [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/). Jakob, Fitts, Hick, Miller, Doherty, Tesler,
    Von Restorff, serial position, peak-end, Zeigarnik, goal gradient, the Gestalt laws,
    chunking, selective attention
- [Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/), Wathan and Schoger.
    The chapter
    list is the rule list: hierarchy is everything, size isn't everything, de-emphasize to
    emphasize, labels are a last resort, start with too much white space, establish a
    spacing system, use fewer borders, don't rely on color alone
- [How we redesigned the Linear UI](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui),
    Linear.
    Fewer chromatic elements, darker neutral text, aligned chrome, density in
    navigation, display and text faces
- [Sentry issue details](https://docs.sentry.io/product/issues/issue-details/).
    The
    shape of an evidence page: header with counts and workflow controls, filters, a
    trend graph with tags beside it, one recommended event with first and last one click
    away, then stackable sections (stack trace, breadcrumbs, trace, replay, tags)
- [incident.io AI SRE](https://incident.io/ai-sre).
    Every hypothesis links to its
    sources, a confidence beside each finding, an adversarial check before sharing, a
    timeline of the investigation, and the reader can ask it questions in place
- Honeycomb BubbleUp (docs moved; the pattern is the one to keep): select the
    outliers on a heatmap and get, per attribute, a histogram of selected against
    baseline, ranked by how much they differ, each drilling to the raw events
